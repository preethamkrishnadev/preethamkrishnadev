The gap I work in

Most enterprise AI dies in the gap between a demo that impresses and a workflow nobody documented. I work in that gap — on site, in the mess, until the thing runs without me.

I'm a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer at Autonomize AI, building agentic platforms for healthcare revenue cycle management alongside stakeholders at Skylight and Molina Healthcare. Before that: ten years of production engineering across insurance-tech, enterprise security, and live broadcast platforms.

The through-line is systems that cannot fail quietly. At Sony Pictures Networks I ran the platforms behind KBC Play Along and Shark Tank India — millions of concurrent players, sub-second answer validation, zero-downtime deploys during live primetime. That instinct for failure modes is what I now bring to agent harnesses, guardrails, and eval pipelines.

I own the whole arc: discovery with the client, architecture, implementation, and the post-deployment loop that turns a demo into something people run their day on. I also lead teams — 6+ engineers, design review, hiring, knowledge transfer.
